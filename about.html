portrait / 2026 — hi.

I'm Adithi, a designer, researcher, and person who gets unreasonably excited about how things feel to use.

I'm a UX & product designer currently looking for full-time roles. My work lives where qualitative research meets product. I care about the why behind every decision, and I don't stop asking questions until the design actually earns its place.

I found design by accident and stayed on purpose. A computer science degree taught me how systems think. HCI taught me how people think. Now I spend my time in the space between, translating real human frustration into interfaces that feel like they were made for you specifically.

“I want to make things that people don't have to think about because someone already thought about it deeply enough for them.”

Outside of design, I'm figuring out adulthood in Germany one cultural shock at a time, filling notebooks with ideas that may or may not be genius, and reading books like it's a personality. I believe in design that includes everyone, not as a principle on a slide, but as the actual starting point.
